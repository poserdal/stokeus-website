## What are relationship rituals?

Rituals are the small, repeated traditions that define your relationship. Morning coffee together. Kissing goodbye before work. Sunday breakfast at the same diner. The way you decompress together after the kids go to sleep.

They're not events. They're patterns. And they matter more than most couples realize.

Gottman found that rituals of connection are essential to relationship health. They're predictable moments where you show up for each other. Even when life is chaotic, the ritual holds.

### Why do rituals matter?

Because relationships run on consistency. Grand gestures are nice. Date nights are fine. But the daily rituals are what create the feeling of being a team.

When you have rituals, you don't have to negotiate connection. It's built into the schedule. You know that at 7am, you'll have coffee together. That at 6pm, you'll take a walk. That before bed, you'll check in.

Without rituals, connection becomes something you have to create from scratch every time. That takes energy. Energy you often don't have.

> Rituals are connection on autopilot. And in a busy life, autopilot is what survives.

### What counts as a ritual?

Anything repeated and meaningful. It doesn't have to be elaborate. It just has to be yours.

How you greet each other when you get home. The show you watch together on Thursdays. The way you say goodnight. Your Saturday morning routine. Your inside jokes. The question you ask at dinner.

The content doesn't matter. The repetition does. The fact that it's something you do together, predictably, that signals "this is us."

### What if we don't have rituals?

Then you need to build some. Not in a heavy way. Not in a "let's schedule our connection" way. Just notice where rituals could live and create them.

Pick a daily transition and make it intentional. Morning. Coming home from work. Before bed. One of those moments can become a ritual. Coffee together. A real hello. A two-minute check-in.

Start small. One ritual is better than none. Once it's established, add another.

### What if our old rituals died?

Life changes. Kids. New jobs. Moves. Rituals that worked before might not fit anymore.

That's normal. The fix isn't to mourn the old rituals. It's to build new ones that fit your current life. The couple you are now probably needs different connection points than the couple you were five years ago.

### Can rituals get stale?

Yes. If a ritual becomes just a routine, it loses meaning. The difference between a ritual and a routine is intention.

If you're having coffee together but you're both on your phones, that's a routine. If you're having coffee together and actually connecting, that's a ritual.

Check in periodically. Is this ritual still working? Are we actually present for it? If not, refresh it or replace it.

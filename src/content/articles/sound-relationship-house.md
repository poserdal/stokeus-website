## What is the Sound Relationship House?

It's Gottman's framework for what makes relationships work. After studying thousands of couples, he distilled everything into a model with seven levels, like floors of a house. Each floor builds on the one below it. Weak floors make everything above them unstable.

This isn't theory. It's what the research actually shows predicts whether couples stay together and stay happy.

### What are the seven floors?

The foundation is building love maps. That's how well you know your partner's inner world. Their fears, dreams, stresses, history. If you don't know them, you can't connect with them.

Second floor: fondness and admiration. Do you still like and respect each other? Without this, everything else falls apart.

Third floor: turning toward. Do you respond to each other's bids for connection? This is where daily connection lives.

Fourth floor: positive perspective. When the first three floors are solid, you give each other the benefit of the doubt. You interpret their actions charitably instead of assuming the worst.

Fifth floor: managing conflict. Not avoiding it. Managing it. Knowing how to navigate disagreements without destroying each other.

Sixth floor: making life dreams come true. Supporting each other's individual goals and aspirations, not just shared ones.

Seventh floor: creating shared meaning. Building rituals, roles, and a sense of purpose as a couple.

> The house is built from the bottom up. You can't have shared meaning without fondness. You can't have fondness without knowing them.

### Which floor is most couples' weakest?

Usually the first three. Love maps get outdated and nobody notices. Fondness erodes into criticism without anyone intending it. Bids for connection get missed because everyone's too busy.

These are the invisible floors. They deteriorate slowly. By the time couples realize something's wrong, they're already dealing with the symptoms on higher floors.

### How do I know which floor needs work?

Ask yourself the diagnostic questions for each level.

Love maps: Can you name their current biggest stress? Their best friend right now? What they're dreading this month?

Fondness and admiration: Can you list three things you genuinely respect about them right now?

Turning toward: When they say something random, do you respond? Or do you half-listen while doing something else?

If any of those feel shaky, that's where to focus.

### How do I strengthen a weak floor?

The same way it got weak: through small repeated actions.

For love maps: ask one real question a week.

For fondness: express one specific appreciation daily.

For turning toward: respond to their bids instead of missing them.

The work isn't dramatic. It's consistent. One floor at a time, one small action at a time.

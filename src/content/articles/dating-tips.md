## What dating tips actually matter?

Most dating tips focus on what to say, where to go, how to text, when to call. That's all surface stuff. It matters a little. But the reason most dating advice doesn't work is because it treats dating like a skill you can optimize. It's not. Dating works when you stop trying to win and start trying to see if this person is actually right for you.

The real dating tips have nothing to do with conversation starters or what to wear. They're about whether you're ready to date at all, and whether you're being honest about what you actually want.

### Should I know what I want before I start dating?

Most people start dating because they feel like they should, or because they're lonely, or because everyone else is doing it. Then they wonder why every date feels exhausting. If you don't know what you're looking for, you're just showing up to see what happens. That's not dating. That's killing time with strangers.

You don't need a list of requirements. You just need to know whether you're looking for something serious or something casual. And you need to be honest about it. Not what you think you should want. What you actually want right now.

### Should I try to be interesting on dates?

The worst dating tip that everyone follows is "be interesting." It makes people perform. They tell the polished version of every story. They name-drop the books they haven't read. They pretend to like hiking. None of it works because the other person can feel the effort.

Interesting is not the goal. Honest is the goal. If you're boring when you're being yourself, you're going to be boring in a relationship too. The right person will not find that boring. They'll find it comfortable. That's what you're looking for. Someone who doesn't make you feel like you need to be more than you are.

> Dating gets easier the second you stop treating it like an audition.

### Should I fix my life before dating?

Here's the thing nobody wants to hear. If your life is a mess, dating will not fix it. If you're miserable at your job, or you have no friends, or you're still not over your ex, going on dates is just going to make all of that worse. You'll be looking for someone to solve problems that are yours to solve.

Get your shit together first. Not perfectly. Just functionally. Have a life you actually like. Then date. Because the goal is to find someone who adds to that life, not someone who becomes the only good part of it.

### Should I trust my gut on a first date?

If it feels off on the first date, it's off. Trust that. You don't owe anyone a second chance. You don't need to wait and see if feelings develop. The whole point of dating is to figure out quickly whether this is worth your time. If the answer is no, say no.

Someone can check every box and still feel wrong in person. That's fine. Let them go. Stop trying to logic your way into attraction. You either feel something or you don't. Compatibility matters, but only after chemistry.

### Why do I care so much about getting rejected?

A lot of dating advice is secretly about reducing rejection. Be confident so they pick you. Say the right thing so they text back. Play it cool so they don't lose interest. All of that is exhausting and it doesn't work anyway.

You're going to get rejected. A lot. That's the whole structure of dating. Most people are not going to be right for you. You're not going to be right for most people. That's fine. The goal is not to trick someone into liking you. It's to find the person who likes you without the tricks.

Be direct. Be yourself. The wrong people will leave faster. That's the point.

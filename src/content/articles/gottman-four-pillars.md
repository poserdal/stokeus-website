## What are the four pillars of trust?

Trust isn't one thing. According to the Gottman framework, it's built on four separate pillars: honesty, transparency, accountability, and reliability. Each one is distinct. Each one can be strong while another is weak.

Understanding which pillar is shaky helps you know what to fix. Telling someone "I don't trust you" isn't actionable. Telling them "I don't trust that you'll follow through" is.

### What is honesty in relationships?

Honesty is telling the truth. Not just avoiding lies, but being truthful about what you feel, what you need, and what's going on.

A lot of couples avoid lying but also avoid truth-telling. They withhold. They minimize. They say "I'm fine" when they're not. That's not dishonesty exactly, but it's not honesty either. Over time, it erodes trust because your partner learns they're not getting the whole picture.

### What is transparency?

Transparency is the willingness to be seen. To share information without being asked for it. To not have hidden compartments.

Honesty means telling the truth when asked. Transparency means volunteering it. It's the difference between "I didn't lie about where I was" and "I told you my plans before you had to wonder."

Couples with good transparency don't have to play detective. They share proactively.

> Honesty is not lying. Transparency is not hiding. Most couples are better at one than the other.

### What is accountability?

Accountability is owning what you did wrong. Without excuses. Without deflection. Without making it about what they did first.

This one is hard. When you mess up, your instinct is to protect yourself. Explain. Justify. But accountability requires setting all that aside and simply saying: "I did that. I was wrong. I'm sorry."

Couples who can't do this get stuck. Every conflict becomes a trial about whose fault it is instead of a repair.

### What is reliability?

Reliability is doing what you said you'd do. Following through. Being consistent over time.

You can be honest, transparent, and accountable, and still unreliable. And reliability might be the most practical pillar. It's hard to trust someone who never does what they promise, even if they apologize sincerely every time.

### Which pillar am I weakest on?

Think about where you get feedback. Does your partner complain that you withhold information? Transparency. That you make excuses? Accountability. That you don't follow through? Reliability. That they can't tell what you're really feeling? Honesty.

Most people are stronger on some pillars than others. The goal isn't perfection. It's knowing where to focus.

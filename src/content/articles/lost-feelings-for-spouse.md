## What does it mean when you've lost feelings for your spouse?

You wake up next to someone you promised forever to and feel nothing. Not anger. Not resentment. Just a flat, terrible nothing. They didn't cheat. They didn't change. You just stopped feeling it, and now you're wondering if you're broken or cruel or both.

The difference between dating and marriage is that when you lose feelings for a boyfriend or girlfriend, you can leave. When you've lost feelings for your spouse, you're staring at shared bank accounts and maybe kids and a life you built together. The question isn't just "do I still love them" but "what kind of person am I if I don't."

### Is something wrong with my spouse or with me?

Most marriage advice will tell you to identify what changed. Did they gain weight? Did they stop trying? That line of thinking is garbage because it assumes someone is at fault. Sometimes nobody did anything wrong and the feeling still left.

Here's what actually happened. You both fell into a routine. The version of them you see every day became so familiar that your brain stopped registering them as a person. You're not looking at your spouse anymore. You're looking at the person who loads the dishwasher wrong.

The question is whether that numbness is protective or permanent. Sometimes your brain shuts down feeling because it's overwhelmed. Sometimes the feeling is just gone. You won't know which one it is until you test it.

### What's the smallest thing I can try?

Don't start with couples therapy or a vacation. Start with something so small it feels stupid. Touch their arm when you walk past them. Text them one thing about your day that isn't logistics. Not "what time are you home," just "saw a dog that looked like the one from that movie we watched."

Pay attention to what happens inside you when you do this. If it feels forced but not repulsive, that's different than if it makes your skin crawl. If they respond warmly and you feel a flicker of something, even annoyance that you feel something, that's information. If you feel nothing and they feel nothing, that's information too.

> Lost feelings for your spouse doesn't make you a bad person. Pretending they're still there when they're not is worse for both of you.

### Is the person I fell for still in there?

You didn't marry a stranger. Somewhere under the routine and the resentment and the boredom, that person still exists. The question is whether you still want to find them.

Think about the moment you realized you wanted to marry them. What were they doing? What were you doing? You can't go back to that moment, but you can try to reconstruct the conditions that made it possible. That means acting like someone you want to know instead of someone you're obligated to live with. One real question per day. That's it.

### When does effort become self-punishment?

There's a difference between trying to rebuild something and forcing yourself to feel something that isn't there. If you've been doing the small things for weeks and it still feels like performing, that matters. If your spouse is meeting you halfway and you still feel nothing, that matters even more.

Some marriages don't survive lost feelings. Not because anyone failed, but because people change and sometimes they change in directions that don't fit anymore. You're allowed to admit that.

### Should I tell my spouse I've lost feelings?

You can't fix this alone. At some point you have to say the actual words to your spouse. Not as an accusation. Just as a fact. "I feel like I've lost feelings for you and I don't know what to do about it."

Their reaction will tell you everything you need to know about whether this is fixable. If they shut down, you have your answer. If they admit they've felt it too, that's a different answer. If they're willing to try, and you're willing to try, then you try.

But if the idea of saying it out loud makes you feel relief instead of fear, listen to that.

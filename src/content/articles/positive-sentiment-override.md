## What is positive sentiment override?

Positive sentiment override is what happens when your overall feeling about your partner is positive enough that you interpret ambiguous behavior charitably. They forgot to call you back. In positive sentiment override, you think: "they must have been busy." In negative sentiment override, you think: "they don't care about me."

Same behavior. Completely different interpretation. And the interpretation determines whether you're starting a fight or letting it go.

Gottman found that couples in positive sentiment override can weather a lot. Their foundation is strong enough that one negative moment doesn't register as a threat.

### How do I know which sentiment override I'm in?

Pay attention to how you interpret their behavior when it's ambiguous. They were short with you this morning. Do you assume they're stressed, or do you assume they're being a jerk?

If your automatic interpretation is charitable, you're in positive override. If it's suspicious or negative, you've flipped.

You can also check by noticing how you describe them to others. Do you tell stories that make them look good or bad? The stories reveal your internal narrative.

> Positive sentiment override means your default is trust. Negative sentiment override means your default is suspicion.

### What causes negative sentiment override?

Accumulated resentment. When you've experienced enough negative interactions without repair, your brain starts classifying your partner as unreliable or unkind. Every new ambiguous moment gets filtered through that lens.

It's not one fight that flips you. It's the pattern. The ratio of positive to negative. If the ratio has been off for long enough, your brain adjusts its expectations.

### Can I flip from negative back to positive?

Yes. But not by deciding to. You can't just choose to interpret things charitably when you don't feel that way. You have to rebuild the foundation.

That means increasing positive interactions. Making repairs after conflict. Following through on promises. Showing up consistently. Over time, as the positive ratio improves, your default interpretation will shift.

You're not changing your mind. You're changing the data your mind uses to form expectations.

### What if I'm in positive override but my partner isn't?

That's common. You're not always on the same page. If you're feeling good about the relationship but they're not, their interpretation of your behavior will be harsher than you expect.

The fix is to be more explicit. Don't assume they'll interpret things charitably. Say what you mean. Explain your intentions. Make it harder for the negative filter to distort things.

### How do I protect positive sentiment override?

Keep the ratio healthy. Five positives for every negative during conflict. More like twenty to one in daily life. Respond to bids. Express appreciation. Repair after fights.

The couples who stay in positive sentiment override aren't lucky. They're doing the maintenance. Every day.

## What questions actually update your love map?

Not "how was your day." That's logistics. A love map is your internal model of who your partner is right now. Their fears, hopes, stresses, dreams. The stuff that changes constantly and that you've probably stopped tracking.

Here are 20 questions that actually matter. You don't need to ask all of them in one sitting. That would be weird. Pick one when the moment feels right.

### Questions about their inner world

- What's been weighing on you lately that you haven't said out loud?
- What are you looking forward to this month?
- What's something you've been putting off because it feels too hard?
- If you had a completely free weekend, what would you actually do?
- What's one thing you wish I understood better about you?

### Questions about your relationship

- What's something I do that makes you feel loved?
- What's something I used to do that you miss?
- Is there anything you've wanted to bring up but haven't?
- What's a small thing I could do this week that would matter to you?
- When do you feel most connected to me?

### Questions about their life outside you

- Who are you closest to right now besides me?
- What's something going on with your family that I should know about?
- What's stressing you out at work that you haven't mentioned?
- Is there a friendship you've been neglecting that you feel bad about?
- What's something you want to do more of but can't find time for?

### Questions about the future

- What are you worried about for the next year?
- What are you excited about?
- Is there something you've been dreaming about that feels too impractical to mention?
- What's something you want us to do together that we haven't?
- If things were different, what would you change about your life right now?

> The point isn't to get through the list. It's to remember that you don't already know the answers.

### Why don't I already know this stuff?

Because you stopped asking. Not maliciously. Life got busy. You started assuming you knew them instead of checking. And they stopped volunteering because you stopped seeming curious.

These questions aren't magic. They're just reminders that your partner's inner world keeps changing and you have to keep up.

### How do I ask these without it feeling forced?

Don't announce that you're doing a relationship exercise. That makes it weird. Just ask one question when you're already talking. At dinner. On a walk. Before bed.

If they ask why you're suddenly curious, tell them the truth. You realized you haven't been asking enough. That's not embarrassing. That's the opposite.

### What if they don't want to answer?

Then they don't. Don't push. The question itself is a signal that you want to know. If they're not ready to share, that's fine. Ask something else another time. The consistency matters more than any single answer.
